Work

Case study: Apeksha Alloys Inn LLP

A steel stockist in Bhosari MIDC that supplies 500+ businesses with carbon, alloy and tool steel cut to size. It is my family's business and my first client.

Data analysis · Process

Stock is checked before every invoice

Before

Invoices were made first and stock was reconciled later. When I analysed their DataScan data, the stock on record did not match the purchase entries.

After

I advised a simple rule: before an invoice is made, the stock for that item is checked against it, and the bill goes ahead only if the material is there.

Result: stock against invoices is now accurate.
